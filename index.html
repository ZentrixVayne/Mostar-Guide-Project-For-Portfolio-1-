Plan Your Walk

Three ways to see the city.

01

The Heritage Loop

Start at Stari Most, cross into the bazaar, visit Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque, and loop back along the river. A slow morning that captures Mostar's Ottoman heart.

02

Layers of Time

Wander from the Old Bridge to Kajtaz House, then sit with the moving images at the War Photo Exhibition. A deep dive into the city's resilience.

03

Riverside Light

Save this for the late afternoon. Walk the banks of the Neretva, watch the bridge divers, and stay for dinner as the stone turns gold and amber.
